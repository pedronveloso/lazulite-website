What's New

2026.10

New

  • Tablet support! Lazulite now makes better use of larger screens.
  • A dedicated Playback health card on Home shows newly observed audio underruns (when an audio buffer runs short of data), dropped outgoing audio buffers, and audio connection problems reported by Android across refreshes during playback.
  • Japanese is now available 🇯🇵.

Improved

  • The A2DP hardware offload section now distinguishes “Available” from “Enabled”. “Enabled” appears only when the platform’s offload setting is on, audio is playing, and the active codec is listed as supported for offload.
  • More reliable resolution of the connected device’s Bluetooth address improves vendor lookup and device details.
  • Device icons now better reflect what you’re listening on, with distinct icons for speakers, headphones, watches, car audio systems, and audio receivers.
  • Battery-level reporting now supports more connected audio devices.

Fixed

  • Several minor bug fixes.

2026.09

  • A refreshed Home screen makes your audio path easier to follow.
  • See which app is playing, with app icons shown on more Android devices.
  • More accurate LDAC decoding on OnePlus devices.
  • Share your preferences and feedback through an optional in-app questionnaire.

2026.08

  • See the complete audio path from the playback app to your Bluetooth device.
  • View the playback app’s output format, sample rate, bit depth, channels, and routing.
  • Spot possible sample-rate conversion.
  • Improved Bluetooth and playback detection.
  • Better support for Android 10, Android 12, and newer Android versions.
  • Fixed compatibility issues on API 29 and some Lenovo devices running API 33.
  • View A2DP Hardware Offload status and supported codecs.
  • Improved LHDC V5 and AAC bitrate decoding.
  • A clearer, redesigned onboarding experience.
  • Better guidance for Bluetooth, notification, and system-log access.
  • Automatic playback updates remain optional.
  • Choose between system, light, and dark themes.
  • Italian is now available.
  • Added a 5-second auto-refresh option.
  • Optionally look up the manufacturer of the connected audio device.
  • Share compressed diagnostic logs more easily.

  • Improved Home screen details display.

  • Several decoder fixes, performance improvements, and app size optimizations.
